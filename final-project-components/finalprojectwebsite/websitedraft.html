Secret Cat Association Agency

For centuries we have connected and lived with cats. For so long we assumed our cats couldn’t understand us. Research from the SCAA or the Secret Cat Association Agency has suggested that our cats understand English, they are just choosing not to respond. This idea changes a lot of things we already know about cats. We know our cats understand when its time to eat, they learn their names at certain point. Through continued research we can slowly start to uncover the truth about how our cats choose to acknowledge us or not.
