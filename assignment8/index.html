Cool Cat Facts

Bone and muscle repairing purr frequency. A cats purr frequency is about 26 cycles per second, the same at which our bones and muscles repair themselves.

Charlottesville Cat Care Clinic says, "Cats use their whiskers to “feel” the world around them in an effort to determine which small spaces they can fit into. A cat’s whiskers are generally about the same width as its body, and this is why you should never, EVER cut their whiskers".
